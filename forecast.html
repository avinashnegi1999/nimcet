Three findings that change what you study

Vectors is gone

Vector & 3D questions ran at 5–8 per paper for 18 years, then collapsed to zero in 2026 when the revised syllabus dropped vector algebra. Those slots moved into Statistics (+2.0), Sets & Functions (+1.6) and Algebra (+1.6) versus their 5-year means.

Questions are recycled

270 cross-year near-duplicates were detected, 77 of them near-verbatim. The gap peaks at 2 years and 4 years — which points 2027 straight at the 2025 and 2023 papers.

What this forecast cannot do

Read this before you trust any number above.

  • No individual question is predicted. The generated 2027 paper reproduces concept, structure and difficulty — not content. Expect concept-level hits, never question-level ones.
  • Small topics are noise. Permutation & combination, number theory and complex numbers sit at 0–3 questions, so their intervals run to zero. Do not over-read them.
  • Two papers are damaged. 2012's image-only pages have now been read (only 2 garbled entries remain), but 2015 stops at Q90 and 2019 has 6 missing questions, so pre-2016 trends carry more uncertainty than the tables suggest.
  • A new syllabus revision would invalidate this. If NIMCET publishes a revised syllabus for 2027, the section split and the vectors call must both be re-checked first.
  • The recycling rate is a floor, not a ceiling. The detector only reads cleanly-extracted text stems — 1,751 of 2,248 questions — so the true repeat rate is higher than 1 in 10.
