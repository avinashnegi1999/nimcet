## 2. Trends

The old pages quoted Mann-Kendall τ and p for single topics (Statistics τ = +0.57, p = 0.002; Puzzles τ = −0.44,
p = 0.012; Algebra τ = −0.34, p = 0.049). The script that produced them is not in the repo; the audit recomputed them
with `scipy.stats.kendalltau` and, because **39 topics** were tested, applied a Benjamini–Hochberg correction:

- **Statistics rising** is the only trend that survives (on question counts τ = +0.60, q = 0.045; on shares q = 0.15).
- Puzzles falling: q = 0.19 — not reliable. Algebra & progressions drifting down: q = 0.40 — noise.
- At chapter level on the 2027 syllabus (69 chapters), nothing is significant at 5% after correction; Statistics rising
  and Problem Solving falling are borderline (q = 0.08).

Fixed in: `forecast.html` (trend paragraph, tier notes), `README.md`, `nimcet-2027-prediction.md` §3 and the
re-verification table, `study-plan-by-tier.md` (S4, A3, A6 notes), the mock paper and question bank notes.

small leak of the answer. Re-scored with only the **official** section sizes (pre-2023: 50/40/10/20; 2023+:
50/40/20/10):

| Method | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| EWMA α = 0.20 (selected) | 1.422 | **1.450** |
| Mean of last 5 | 1.429 | 1.451 |
| Mean of all years | 1.440 | 1.466 |
| EWMA α = 0.35 | 1.442 | 1.469 |
| EWMA α = 0.50 | 1.501 | 1.527 |
| Same as last year | 1.829 | 1.845 |

The ranking is unchanged. `forecast.html` now says ±1.45 (was ±1.4) and "1.45 vs 1.85".
[`scripts/forecast.py`](scripts/forecast.py) was updated to score this way (and to drop the duplicate printings in §4),
so it prints exactly this table.

## 9. New: 2027-syllabus chapter forecast

[`syllabus-2027-heatmap.html`](../syllabus-2027-heatmap.html) ranks every chapter of the 2027 syllabus by its chance of
appearing in NIMCET 2027. Every one of the 2,248 questions was placed in a 2027-syllabus chapter (299 hand
corrections to a rule-based draft, each with its reason). The model was chosen by a rolling back-test over 2016–2026: Brier score 0.1635
vs 0.2497 for a flat guess. Its own audit, also done today:

- the same section-size leak as §3 — fixed;
- the expected-count range had been labelled "80%" but held the real count 91% of the time — relabelled;
- a claim that each syllabus unit has a fixed question quota was not supported by a permutation test — rewritten;
- labels corrected: 2023 Q43 → Statistics, 2024 Q105 → CPU & Instructions; 2018 Q85 excluded as a duplicate.

Method, data and scripts: [`syllabus-2027/`](syllabus-2027/README.md). `index.html` and `forecast.html` now link to it.
