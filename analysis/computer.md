## What repeats
**Number System & Boolean Logic = 129 questions (6.8/yr, nearly every year).** Binary/octal/hex conversion, 1's & 2's complement, Boolean algebra, logic gates, K-maps. Small closed syllabus, near-guaranteed marks — the best ROI topic in the entire paper.

**Hardware, OS & General CS** is trending up hard (2 → 10 in last 5 years). Worth a memorization pass.

## Study order
1. **Number System & Boolean Logic** — master fully, it's basically free marks.
2. **Hardware, OS & General CS** — rising fast, do a fact-sheet grind.
3. **Skip early:** Networking, Programming/DS — ~1 Q/yr combined, mostly absent. Last-week skim only.
