## The one that repeats hardest
**Logical Deduction & Puzzles = 194 questions, the single most-frequent topic in the whole exam** (10.2/yr, every year). No syllabus to learn — pure pattern practice. Highest ROI target in Reasoning.

## Study order
1. **Logical Deduction & Puzzles** — biggest bucket, drill daily.
2. **Seating, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations** — 2.6–3.6/yr each, learnable to near-100%.
3. **Syllogism, Clocks & Calendars** — quick rule-based topics.
4. **Skim last:** Data Interpretation, Analogy, Direction Sense, Odd-one-out, Cubes/Dice — sporadic (<1/yr, seen in <half the years).
