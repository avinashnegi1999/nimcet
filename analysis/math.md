## What repeats every single year (never skip)
The top 7 topics — Calculus, Trigonometry, Algebra & Progressions, Coordinate Geometry, Arithmetic, Vectors & 3D, Probability — each show up **every year**. Together ≈ **38 questions/year**, about a third of the whole paper. These 7 alone decide the Math score.

## Study order
1. **Calculus + Trigonometry + Algebra** first — ~21 Q/yr combined, all guaranteed.
2. **Coordinate Geometry + Arithmetic + Vectors/3D** next — ~17 Q/yr, all guaranteed.
3. **Probability + P&C** — ~8 Q/yr, high overlap in method.
4. Cover Sets/Functions, Statistics, Matrices, Number Theory after.
5. **Skim last:** Complex Numbers, Differential Equations — ~1 Q/yr combined. Not worth early time.
