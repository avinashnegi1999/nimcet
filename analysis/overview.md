## 3. Priority plan (freq × YOUR weakness = Math)

### 🔴 Tier 1 — master first (high freq + your weak section)
All Math, all appear ~every year. This is where your 600→800 jump lives.
1. **Calculus** — 7.7/yr, every year
2. **Trigonometry** — 6.8/yr, every year
3. **Algebra & Progressions** — 6.7/yr, every year
4. **Coordinate & Conic Geometry** — 5.9/yr, every year
5. **Arithmetic** (speed/time/work, ratio, %, mixture) — 5.9/yr
6. **Vectors & 3D** — 5.2/yr, every year
7. **Probability** — 4.7/yr, every year
8. **Permutation & Combination** — 3.6/yr

> These 8 topics = **~40 questions/year** = a third of the whole paper, and it's your weak section. Non-negotiable.

### 🟡 Tier 2 — big scoring buckets outside Math (fast ROI)
9. **Logical Deduction & Puzzles** — 10.2/yr — *single biggest topic in the exam.* Pure practice, no syllabus.
10. **Number System & Boolean Logic** (Computer) — 6.8/yr — small syllabus, near-guaranteed marks.
11. **Vocabulary** (syn/antonym) — 4.9/yr — flashcard grind.
12. **Seating & Arrangement**, **Series**, **Coding-Decoding**, **Blood Relations** — 2.6–3.6/yr each, all learnable to near-100%.
13. **Fill in Blanks + Grammar** — 6/yr combined, easy English marks.

### 🟢 Tier 3 — cover after Tier 1&2, don't obsess
Sets/Functions, Statistics, Matrices, Syllogism, Comprehension, Idioms, Clocks/Calendars, Number Theory.

### ⚪ Tier 4 — skim / last-week only (rare, ≤0.5/yr)
Complex Numbers, Differential Equations, Programming/DS, Networking, Cubes/Dice, Analogy, Odd-one-out, Data Interpretation, Direction Sense.

## 4. Blunt conclusions

- **Math + Logical Puzzles + Number System** alone = **~24 questions/year** from just 3 focus areas. Nail these before anything else.
- Your bottleneck (Math) overlaps perfectly with the highest-frequency topics — good news, effort compounds.
- **Skip early:** complex numbers, differential equations, programming/DS. Combined ~1 Q/year. Not worth Tier-1 time.
- Reasoning is your cheapest points/hour after Math basics — deduction puzzles show up 10×/year and need zero theory.
