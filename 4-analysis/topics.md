## Mathematics (50 questions a paper)

- **Asked in every paper:** Calculus, Trigonometry, Algebra & Progressions, Coordinate geometry, Probability, and Sets & Functions. P&C missed only 2022. Together they make up about 37 of the 50.
- **Vectors & 3D are gone.** There were 0 in 2026 and they are off the syllabus.
- **Statistics is the only rising trend** that survives a multiple-testing correction.
- **Mathematical Logic is new.** It had 1 question each in 2023 and 2026, and the syllabus names it.
