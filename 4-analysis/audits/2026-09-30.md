# Re-audit — 2026-09-30

Scope: whether the analysis (data, labels, re-use counts, trends) is right, and whether the two 2027 forecasts are
built correctly — the topic forecast ([`forecast.html`](../../forecast.html), `analysis/scripts/forecast.py`) and the
2027-syllabus chapter forecast ([`syllabus-2027-heatmap.html`](../../syllabus-2027-heatmap.html),
[`syllabus-2027/scripts/`](../../scripts/chapter-forecast/)).

Method: every pipeline was re-run from scratch in a separate copy before anything was changed, every headline number was
re-derived from `data/`, a random sample of questions was read against its labels, and the syllabus was checked against
the official document. The forecast itself can only be checked against the real 2027 paper; what can be checked now is
whether it is built correctly and whether its claims about itself are honest.

## 2. What was wrong, and the fix

### 2.1 Computer forecast was biased (both forecasts)

Computer doubled from 10 to 20 questions in 2023 and its mix changed with it. Forecasting from all 19 papers kept
over-predicting number systems and under-predicting hardware/OS, in the same direction every year:

| Target paper | Number System & Boolean: predicted / asked | Hardware, OS & General CS: predicted / asked |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2023 | 13.1 / 12 | 6.5 / 8 |
| 2024 | 12.9 / 9 | 6.8 / 9 |
| 2025 | 12.2 / 8 | 7.3 / 8 |
| 2026 | 11.5 / 5 | 7.5 / 9 |

The chapter forecast showed the same thing at unit level (Data Representation 13.3/12, 13.1/9, 12.7/9, 12.4/5).

**Topic forecast fix** (`forecast.py`): from 2024 on, Computer is forecast from the 20-question papers only (plain mean
of shares). Back-test on the 2024–2026 Computer topics: 1.97 questions per topic vs 2.45 for the all-years EWMA; only
"same as last year" scored lower (1.92) and it is too jumpy to use (it would forecast 6 networking and 5 number-system
questions). English also changed size in 2023, but there the all-years EWMA still back-tests best (1.19 on 2024–2026),
so English is unchanged. Overall back-test error 2015–2026: 1.450 → **1.437**.

| 2027 Computer forecast | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Number System & Boolean Logic | 10.2 | **8.7** |
| Hardware, OS & General CS | 7.8 | **8.7** |
| Networking & Internet | 1.2 | **1.5** |
| Programming & data structures | 0.8 | **1.1** |

**Chapter forecast fix** (`05_backtest.js`, `06_forecast.js`): using only 2023+ papers for every Computer chapter made the
chapter chances worse (Brier 0.208 vs 0.148 on 2024–2026 — too little data per chapter). The hybrid that was adopted
keeps each chapter's share *within its unit* from the weighted full history and takes each **unit's total** from the
2023+ papers. On 2024–2026 Computer chapters: MAE 0.618 vs 0.655, Brier 0.150 vs 0.148 (no real change). Whole back-test
2016–2026: MAE 0.858 → 0.855, calibrated Brier 0.1635 → 0.1637, range coverage 91.4% (unchanged). Non-Computer
chapters keep their expected counts; their calibrated chances moved by at most 0.2 points because the calibration is
refitted.

| 2027 Computer unit | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Data Representation | 11.5 | **9.3** |
| Computer Basics | 4.9 | **5.8** |
| Computer Hardware | 1.9 | **2.2** |
| Computer Software | 0.9 | **1.3** |
| Internet & Email | 0.9 | **1.5** |

Knock-on changes: Tier S number systems 10 → 9 Q (Tier S ≈ 530 marks, was 540); Tier A architecture/OS 8 → 9 Q;
"biggest single block in the paper" removed (2026 had 5 number-system questions and 13 arithmetic ones); `computer.md`
said "10–12 in 2023–2025" — it was 12, 9, 8; "8–10 per paper since 2023" for hardware/OS was 8–9.

**Still uncertain:** 2026 is the only paper set from the new syllabus. It asked 6 Internet & Email questions against a
forecast of 1.5, and Online Security, application software and utility programs are in the syllabus with no past
questions. The pages now say to cover them anyway (small, factual).

### 2.2 "The method won the back-test" was a tie

EWMA α = 0.20 (1.450), last-5 mean (1.451) and all-years mean (1.466) are indistinguishable: paired difference EWMA −
last-5 = −0.001 ± 0.024 questions over 457 topic-year predictions. Only "same as last year" (1.845) is clearly worse
(−0.395 ± 0.065). Reworded in `README.md`, `forecast.html` and `nimcet-2027-prediction.md` §5.

### 2.3 The topic "80% band" covered about 73%

Out of sample (targets 2019–2026, band width from earlier years only), ±1.28 residual SDs held the real count 73.4% of
the time. `forecast.py` now uses ±1.7 SDs, which holds it 80.4% of the time. All bands in `data/forecast-2027.json`,
the `forecast.html` bars and grid, `nimcet-2027-prediction.md` §7–§8 and the mock-paper headers were regenerated.
(The chapter forecast's "likely range" was already honest at ~91% and is unchanged.)

### 2.4 Chapter forecast compared with a weak baseline

"≈35% better than a flat guess" is true, but the flat guess gives every chapter the same 52% chance. Against the plain
rule "how often was this chapter asked before" (Brier 0.170), the model is ≈4% better. The heat map page and
`syllabus-2027/README.md` now say both.

### 2.5 Syllabus source

Pages said the syllabus PDF was "compiled by NIMCET Aspirant — check it against the official notification". Now
checked: the official revised syllabus (in force from 2026) matches it. `README.md`, `forecast.html`, the heat map page,
`syllabus-2027/README.md` and `nimcet-2027-prediction.md` §11b cite the official PDF.

### 2.6 Data gap not listed

2012 Q63 is a broken image in the source PDF (page 36), so it is not in the data and 2012 has 119 questions. Added to
the known-gaps notes.
