## Five facts that shape the plan

- **The paper is 50 / 40 / 20 / 10.** Math, Reasoning, Computer, English. It has been this way since 2023.
- **Vectors are gone.** There were 0 vector questions in 2026, and the [official syllabus](syllabus/nimcet-syllabus-official-2026.pdf) no longer lists them.
- **Computer is worth twice English.** It has 20 questions, and most of them are factual or mechanical.
- **Arithmetic is in Reasoning.** Ratio, percentages, ages and work problems make up about 9 of the 40 Reasoning questions.
- **Old questions rarely come back now.** 15% of the 2023 paper was re-used, against 0.8% of the 2026 paper.
